# 102049 · Pet Shop — catálogo e reserva (client app)

Part of **collab.codes**.

`102049` is a **generated client application** (`projectType: "client"`,
~95 `.ts` files) for a pet shop storefront.

> **petShop** — *"Este módulo oferece uma vitrine digital e catálogo completo de
> produtos do pet shop, permitindo que clientes pesquisem, filtrem e reservem
> produtos para retirada presencial na loja. O atendente gerencia as reservas
> recebidas, confirma a disponibilidade e registra o pagamento presencial no
> momento da retirada."*
> Business domain: Pet Retail. Language: pt-BR.

Search and filter by pet type, category, name and price range; featured products;
reserve online, pay in store.

## Layout

`l4/petShop/` solution model (`module.defs.ts`, `siteMap.defs.ts`, `actors.defs.ts`,
`ontology/`, `journeys/`, `rules/`, `operations/`, `workflows/`, `contracts/`,
`workspaces/`, `pipeline/`) · `l1/petShop/` backend · `l2/petShop/web/` frontend ·
`l5/petShop/` process and todo defs · `l4/trace/` and `l2/trace/` generation traces.

Last activity: 2026-07-16.

## Careful

Not to be confused with [`102047`](../mls-102047), whose `petShopAgendamento`
module is about **scheduling services**, not selling products.
